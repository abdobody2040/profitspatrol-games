# Batch 09 Asset Notes

## Fallback-reference search

The image-generation quota was unavailable for Batch 09. A visual-reference search returned storybook and isometric business-city samples from StockCake, Vecteezy, Dreamstime, and VectorStock. These results are **reference-only** and are not used in the product because their licensing and reuse terms were not verified.

The Batch 09 interface will therefore retain the established ProfitsPatrol generated-world visuals, distinctive district silhouettes, CSS-based landmark cues, and existing project-owned neighborhood artwork until original generated assets can be created after the quota resets.
