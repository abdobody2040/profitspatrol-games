# Batch 10 Launch City asset note

## Original-art status

An original Launch City reference illustration was requested for Batch 10, but the available image-generation quota was exhausted before an asset could be produced.

## Approved fallback

Batch 10 will preserve the project-owned ProfitsPatrol visual language with its existing storybook neighborhood hero, a dedicated `batch10-map.css` stylesheet, distinctive CSS-built Sales Street, Marketing Studio, Digital Marketplace, Pitch Arena, and Founder Tower silhouettes, and the existing pattern fallback used by stations without bespoke artwork. No third-party image will be added, and no external reference image will be reused without verified rights.

## Follow-up option

When original asset generation becomes available again, an owned Launch City district illustration can replace the pattern fallback without changing the GameRegistry, shared GameShell, saved progress, gameplay rules, or route structure.
